About Me

I am a Metallurgical and Materials Engineering graduate from Universitas Indonesia. My expertise spans materials processing and defect mitigation, built through direct experience across manufacturing operations and large-scale student leadership.

At leading manufacturing firms—PT Astra Honda Motor (AHM), PT Pindad, and PT Unipack Plasindo—I developed a sharp investigative mindset, applying structured methodologies like Root Cause Analysis (RCA) and Fault Tree Analysis (FTA) to resolve production defects and strengthen Quality Assurance (QA/QC) processes.

Beyond the technical side, I've established a track record of leadership—serving as Vice President for the Rumah Kepemimpinan Scholarship and leading initiatives that reached 2,000+ students and volunteers across academic and social programs. These experiences sharpened the cross-functional coordination and communication skills I now bring to technical and operational work alike.
